# Bestiary — All 65 Species

⚠️ **REWRITTEN 2026-08-07 FOR THE APPEARANCE-FIRST IDENTITIES.** Every species was renamed from
the creature actually on screen — its CC0 pack model plus its authored `SPECIES_TINT` — after
the user's call: *"we will have to change the species and names based on appearance."* Internal
ids are stable (saves, contracts, `PACK_MODEL` and the tint table all key on id); the OLD names
survive only as ids. `src/species.ts` is the source of truth for names and one-line flavour;
`src/bestiary.ts` carries the condensed in-game bios; this doc is the canonical long-form lore.

⚠️ **Element resist/weak lines are GONE from this doc** — elements were removed from the game
on 2026-07-30 (`CLAUDE.md` §Body Types). The per-family training aptitude (minor) and each
species' major/flaw are real and current (`Species.trainingProfile`).

**The seven arcs survived the recast.** Displacement, the scattering, the itinerant fair, the
deep stirring, the fading nests, the long wait, and the Lab's first-age fusions are world lore —
they describe what is happening to the world, not what the creatures look like — so the new
creatures stepped into the old stories.

## Mammals — *sturdy, grounded; minor STR*
### Arc: Displacement & Renewed Purpose
The working world stopped needing its strongest backs — guardians replaced by engines, ranges
crossed by roads, troupes scattered by quieter times. The Circuit is where the displaced found
the one thing the new world still cannot manufacture: something worth standing in front of.

### Gruulk (`kongrath`) — green-hided, blue-crested colossus · Warrior · balanced · Chest Beat / Rising Fury
Grew up the biggest, quietest member of a wandering troupe of smaller, mismatched creatures who
banded together for safety on the road. Nothing approached the green colossus twice, and he never asked to be
their protector — he was simply the one standing in front. When the troupe finally scattered,
he found he missed having something worth standing between, and joined the Circuit looking for
it all over again. The first Chest Beat of a match is him announcing he has found it.

### Terrock (`aegisox`) — quarry-tan stone giant · Tank · major CON / flaw DEX · Ironclad / Immovable
Stood a mountain pass for a working lifetime — a squared, quarry-tan wall the caravans planned
their routes around. When the caravans traded living guardians for engines, the pass no longer
needed a wall, and a wall is all Terrock ever wanted to be. The arena still needs one. She
fights to shield her teammates more than to win; Immovable is a job description.

### Manegold (`maneleo`) — tawny-gold shag-beast, hood like a mane · Captain · major CHA / flaw CON · Rallying Roar / Pride
Led a company of prize-fighters until a younger rival talked it out from under him. Refusing
exile, he walked onto the Circuit to rebuild his name from nothing — first into the line,
loudest at the Rallying Roar, gathering a new company one teammate at a time.

### Grynt (`grivvel`) — wiry green scrapper · Rogue · major DEX / flaw CHA · Rend / Frenzy
His scrubland territory shrank a little more every season until the wild simply left him
behind. The Circuit is the first place in years with honest, deliberate boundaries, and he
treats every one of them as negotiable. Already picking the next fight before the last one has
cooled.

### Frostmaul (`ursath`) — blue-white shag-armed mauler · Warrior · major WIS / flaw INT · Thick Hide / Maul
Woke from a long mountain sleep to find his high forest felled and paved. Wanders the Circuit
half in search of a new home, half for the honest satisfaction of a Maul landing true — and the
crowd has learned to love the unhurried way he winds one up.

## Avians — *swift, keen; minor WIS*
### Arc: Scarcity, Migration, and the Long Way Back
A generation ago the roosts emptied — drought, fractured ice, hungry seasons — and the flocks
scattered across a continent of empty sky. Now word travels that the old grounds are safe
again, and the scattered are finding their way back changed: archivists, wanderers, ambushers,
voices that learned to carry.

### Capling (`pinguox`) — small teal cap-headed diver · Ranger · major CON · Dive Bomb / Keen Eye
Born mid-trek, in a generation that never saw the colony her parents fled. Word has finally
come back that the old grounds are safe, and she is testing every plunge of the long journey
home on the Circuit — Dive Bomb lands a little truer with each attempt, and she is cheerfully
unbothered that everything she fights is bigger than she is.

### Aeristra (`strixil`) — pale-blue drifting floater · Sage · major INT / flaw STR · Wellspring / Silent Wisdom
Stayed behind when the scattering emptied her roost, drifting the old thermals alone and
keeping its knowledge alive for whoever found their way back. Now the drifters trickle home in
twos and threes, and she has become the archivist they all quietly rely on — Silent Wisdom is
not a technique, it is a habit that never broke.

### Azurefin (`balaenix`) — deep-blue drifter, pale fins like sails · Rogue · major STR / flaw CHA · Ambush Strike / Statue Stance
Born in backwaters the others abandoned mid-scattering, and stayed rather than follow — the
stillness suited her. She hangs motionless until the moment stops being one: Statue Stance for
an hour, Ambush Strike once. The crowd never sees it coming, which is the point of the crowd,
as far as she is concerned.

### Cobalon (`corvaan`) — cobalt bird-thing with scholarly brows · Wizard · balanced · Arcane Bolt / Hex
Learned to make a home out of nowhere in particular during the long scattering, picking up
trinkets and a stolen spellbook from every territory the wind blew him through. Now that the
old roosts call everyone back, he finds he would rather collect reputation than a nest — every
Arcane Bolt is a page of that book, cast at somebody.

### Rosewing (`larkessa`) — rose-tinted songwing · Bard · major CHA / flaw INT · Song of Valor / Encore
Her voice became more than talent during the scattering — flung far enough on the wind, it
helped the separated find each other across a continent of empty sky. The roosts are full
again, and when she opens a match with the Song of Valor, the applause still reminds her that
every one of those voices made it home.

## Marsupials — *charismatic, agile; minor CHA*
### Arc: The Itinerant Fair
The fair never stops moving, and neither do its people — show-fighters, crooners, mascots and
menaces, raised between one pitch and the next. Some come to the Circuit for real competition
after a life of choreography; some bring the fair with them wherever they stand.

### Mosshorn (`bruxaroo`) — green brawler crowned with mossy horns · Captain · major STR / flaw INT · Haymaker / Southpaw
Made his name in staged fairground exhibition bouts and grew tired of the choreography. Came to
the Circuit hungry for real competition and the honest thud of an unrehearsed Haymaker — the
grin after it lands is the only part of the old act he kept.

### Slumbra (`koalio`) — grey-blue climber, calm to the point of sleep · Orator · major CON / flaw STR · Drowsy Aura / Soothing Words
Has traveled with more fairground troupes than he can name, a familiar stranger wherever the
fair sets up next. Competes to prove gentleness can win — he would genuinely rather ease an
opponent to the floor inside his Drowsy Aura than strike them, and his record says the floor is
patient enough for both.

### Tannik (`quokkade`) — small warm-tan critter · Bard · balanced · Cheer / Quickstep
The fair circuit's most relentlessly cheerful competitor — fights for the crowds, the
friendships, and the picnic afterwards, in roughly that order. Every arena gets the same Cheer,
because every arena, as far as Tannik is concerned, is the same wonderful place.

### Leafwisp (`sylvaglide`) — leaf-green wisp · Ranger · major WIS · Glide Strike / Aerial
Grew up the smallest member of a fair too used to moving fast to wait for stragglers, and
learned early to ride draughts nobody else could feel. She has spent her whole career turning
being underestimated into an ambush — the Glide Strike arrives from an angle that was empty a
moment ago.

### Razzhorn (`tazzik`) — small dark-red demon of pure appetite · Rogue · major DEX / flaw CON · Devour / Whirlwind
Every fair he has traveled with learns to read the signs and start packing a little early. The
arena is the only place that can absorb his full appetite — Devour is not a metaphor — and he
is fiercely, secretly loyal to anyone who can weather it and grin back.

## Aquatics — *wise, ancient; minor INT*
### Arc: The Deep Stirs
Something old is moving in the trenches, and the surface is learning about it one arrival at a
time. Most of the deep fled the strangeness. The ones on the Circuit are the ones who swam
toward it.

### Oozehorn (`maelurk`) — horned green ooze · Wizard · balanced · Ink Cloud / Tentacle Barrage
One of the first to feel the old trenches stir, and unlike most of what lives down there, he
moved toward the strangeness instead of away from it. Surfaced out of pure curiosity, and
treats every Tentacle Barrage as an experiment he is enjoying immensely.

### Coralux (`nautilux`) — shell-orange drifter of a thousand tides · Spellshield · major CON / flaw STR · Ward / Spiral Shell
A thousand-tide drifter who has weathered every storm, including whatever is moving in the deep
dark now. Competes not to conquer but to endure, taking slow, deep pride in a Ward no storm has
ever broken.

### Gravemaw (`carcharun`) — slab-shouldered slate beast · Sage · major STR / flaw CHA · Tidal Wisdom / Weathered Hide
Surfaced long before anyone spoke of the trenches stirring, and has watched entire leagues rise
and crumble since. Returns each season to mentor the young monsters now following him up from
the dark, and to remind an impatient Circuit that Weathered Hide beats fresh fury — patience
outlasts power.

### Veilfin (`mantaris`) — soft-violet glider of the open water · Ranger · major DEX / flaw CON · Wing Current / Current Rider
One of the first in the deep to feel the stirring, and unlike his kin he glided up toward it. A
showman, curious and unbothered by a crowd — hooked on the hush right before the Wing Current
sweeps a whole line off balance.

### Lumigel (`lanterix`) — soft pink glow-blob · Spellsword · major WIS / flaw DEX · Spellblade / Abyssal Glow
Born in true darkness, far below where the stirring first began, with nothing but his own glow
for company. He carries that deep light to the surface now, drawn up by the same restlessness
moving through the trenches — the Abyssal Glow is a small piece of the abyss that followed him
into the ring.

## Insectoids — *tireless, chitinous; minor CON*
### Arc: Decline & New Nests
The great old colonies are winding down — dams finished, temples unvisited, queens outgrown by
their own hives. And in the territories they left empty, new nests are quietly appearing,
raising a generation that never learned to be humble about it.

### Scarabron (`scarabrute`) — bronze-shelled juggernaut · Tank · balanced · Chitin Plate / Burrow
Spent a century as the keystone of a dam his colony built, holding back a river with his own
shell. When the colony finished their work and moved on, he found the Circuit — the only place
left where something is allowed to hit him as hard as the river did, and where Chitin Plate is
still a calling rather than a curiosity.

### Bristleye (`mantevoke`) — green thorn-crowned orb, one unblinking eye · Rogue · major STR / flaw INT · Ambush / Serrated Claws
Guarded a temple garden fewer visitors bother to find anymore — one unblinking watch, perhaps
the last of his line still keeping the old discipline. Competes to test whether that discipline
still means anything in a world that has stopped watching. The Ambush says it does.

### Pincerax (`arachnyx`) — scarlet-shelled scuttler, armour plate and claws · Wizard · major WIS / flaw DEX · Web Trap / Venom Fang
Has never once left her lair to fight — opponents are escorted in past the armour plate stacked
like trophies. Reports of new nests appearing in territories long empty have left the old
duellist, for the first time, curious about the wider world instead of dismissive of it.

### Vesperon (`vespera`) — yellow-and-black songwing queen · Orator · major CHA / flaw STR · Hive Command / Royal Jelly
A queen whose hive grew so vast and self-sufficient it no longer needed her orders — exactly
the kind of new order the old colonies are quietly giving way to. She entered the Circuit to
feel needed again, and Hive Command still carries: ten thousand loyal wings follow her into
every arena anyway.

### Duskdart (`odonatra`) — dusk-cyan skimmer · Ranger · major DEX / flaw WIS · Skim Dart / Compound Eyes
Hatched in one of the new nests quietly appearing where the old territories fell empty — young,
fast, and utterly unbothered by whatever came before her. Flew through a tournament grounds by
accident, dodged everything thrown at her on the way out, and came back the next season to do
it on purpose.

## Reptilians — *patient, armoured; minor DEX*
### Arc: The Long Wait Ends
Cold blood keeps its own calendar. The reptilians spent lifetimes waiting — in bogs, on warm
rocks, inside shells, behind prophecies — and the Circuit is the thing that finally made the
waiting worth ending.

### Mirejaw (`crocmaw`) — olive-drab saurian, low as a bog · Warrior · major STR / flaw WIS · Death Roll / Armored Scales
Waited motionless in his bog for so long that maps marked the ridge of his back as terrain. The
Circuit is the first thing in decades that made him move — and when the Death Roll finally
turns, the arena floor files the same complaint the bog used to.

### Regalor (`iguanor`) — gold-and-ivory grandee · Captain · major INT / flaw CON · Sun Basking / Crest Display
Deposed no one and conquered nothing — a retinue simply formed around him because he looks
magnificent at rest, and then it waited, for years, doing nothing in particular. He competes to
finally prove the crown he never asked for was deserved, and the Crest Display suggests he has
stopped doubting it himself.

### Sandsaur (`serpwyn`) — sand-yellow saurian, moves like heat-haze · Sage · major WIS / flaw STR · Cold Blood / Hypnotic Gaze
Read the futures of champions for forty seasons before deciding to test whether prophecy is
easier from inside the arena. Has never been surprised by an opponent. Not once — ask anyone
caught in the Hypnotic Gaze.

### Emberimp (`geckari`) — small ember-orange clinger · Rogue · balanced · Wall Runner / Tail Drop
Grew up scaling the arena's outer walls to watch matches for free, long before he had a way in.
Fights the way he watched — from angles nobody thinks to defend, which is what Wall Runner has
always really meant.

### Mosscap (`tortavos`) — moss-green domed elder · Spellshield · major CON / flaw INT · Shell Ward / Inner Calm
Signed up for the Circuit two hundred years ago and arrived last spring, entirely unbothered.
Time moves differently under the dome, and opponents who rush the old king tend to find that
out the hard way — Inner Calm is not a state of mind, it is a climate.

## Primevals — *the prestige fusion: first-age titans remade in the Lab*
### Arc: What the Lab Woke Up
Fuse a Mythical frame with the old prestige bloodlines and what wakes up is not new — it is
first-age, and it remembers. The Primevals are the Lab's proof that the dawn of the world can
be reassembled, and the Circuit is where the reassembled go to reclaim it.

### Magnos (`aeonrex`) — amber elder giant · Warrior · Dawn Fury / Unbroken Age
When a Mythical frame is fused with dragon-blood, what wakes up remembers the dawn age — and
resents having missed the ages since. Treats every arena he enters as territory reclaimed, and
Dawn Fury as the paperwork.

### Vorastra (`stellavore`) — devourer in magenta and olive, horns like galaxies going wrong · Ranger · Starving Aim / Light Drinker
The old stories say the gaps between constellations are bite marks. Vorastra neither confirms
nor denies, but opponents report the arena lights dimming when the Light Drinker draws.

### Bronzecap (`chronoshell`) — bronze-domed crowned elder · Spellshield · Era Shell / Time Dilation
An era is sealed inside the dome — tamers who press an ear to it swear they hear weather. Does
not dodge; inside the Era Shell, the moment simply hasn't arrived where the blow lands.

### Ossari (`originmage`) — the first mage, reduced to bone · Wizard · First Spell / Wellspring Eternal
The first spell ever cast never actually ended — it has just been looking for a caster sturdy
enough to keep casting it. The Lab finally built one out of what remains when everything else
about a mage burns away: bone and craft and nothing else, still working.

### Songlume (`worldsong`) — ivory radiance in the shape of a spirit · Bard · Genesis Chord / Ending Verse
The song that shaped the world lost its singer eons ago. Songlume — glowing from within — picks
up the melody mid-verse with the Genesis Chord, and arenas fall silent to let her finish.

## Draconics — *prestige body, Special License*
### Arc: The Licensed Fire
The Old Flights kept their own courts in the high wastes for centuries and considered the
Circuit beneath them — until the crowds grew too large to ignore. The licence system is the
treaty: each court sends an emissary, the guilds get their spectacle, and everyone pretends the
arrangement could survive either side changing its mind.

### Pyrodrake (`pyraxon`) — ember-red dragon · Warrior · major STR / flaw DEX · Flame Aura / Draconic Pride
The ember court's emissary. Banks the arena in heat — the first row learns to lean back — and
treats every match as a diplomatic incident he intends to win. Draconic Pride is not an innate
so much as a condition of his attendance.

### Glacidrake (`frostwyren`) — dragon glazed in glacier-blue · Wizard · major INT / flaw DEX · Blizzard / Glacial Wisdom
The glacier court's scholar, older than the licence system that permits her to fight and
unimpressed by it. Frost gathers on the rails when she enters. She competes the way she
studies: patiently, thoroughly, with a Blizzard held in reserve for anyone who mistakes
patience for hesitation.

### Voltdrake (`stormlerath`) — storm-violet dragon · Ranger · major DEX / flaw CON · Overload / Dodge Storm
The storm court sent its fastest — he arrives a heartbeat before his own thunder and leaves
before the echo settles. Finds the Circuit's rules quaint and its crowds intoxicating; the
Dodge Storm is him showing off to both at once.

### Verdrake (`verdantdrake`) — verdant-green dragon · Sage · major CON / flaw STR · Life Bloom / Root Grasp
The green court alone voted against attending, and Verdrake came anyway — the forest's answer
to a siege, sent to make sure the other flights behave. Fights rooted, Life Bloom mending
whatever the match tears, and has quietly become the reason the draconic licence has never
been revoked.

### Voidfiend (`voidmaw`) — blue-black horned devourer · Wizard · major INT / flaw CON · Void Pulse / Entropy
The courts do not speak of a fifth flight, which is exactly the kind of silence Voidfiend
enjoys occupying. Holds a legal licence, files immaculate paperwork, radiates Entropy — and
nobody at the licensing guild can remember who signed the approval.

## Abyssals — *prestige body, Special License*
### Arc: What Stirred
The Aquatics felt the trenches stir; the Abyssals are the stirring. When the deep began sending
things up, the guilds faced a choice: bar the abyss from the sport, or give it a stage and keep
it where the stewards can see it. The Special License is that decision written in triplicate —
the deep given a stage before it takes one.

### Duskgeist (`tenebrae`) — bat-eared shade in deep violet · Rogue · major DEX / flaw CON · Cloak of Shadow / Silent Strike
First through the door when the licence opened. The light around it gives up early; it fights
from the Cloak of Shadow and signs autographs afterwards, which unsettles people more than the
fighting does.

### Skulgeist (`abyssomancer`) — night-purple shroud, bone-white skull face · Wizard · major INT / flaw CON · Rift Magic / Mana Theft
Drifts in a shroud of night-purple, a skull where a face should be, and casts through a Rift
the licensing inspectors have politely declined to look inside. The Mana Theft is legal. The
guild checked. Twice.

### Morgrel (`lurkerss`) — green shambler · Sage · major WIS / flaw STR · Psychic Aura / Ancient Knowing
Should not still be moving, and moves anyway — walked out of the deepest trench carrying an
Ancient Knowing no one taught it. Patient, endless, and unfailingly courteous, which the
Circuit has decided to find charming rather than think about.

### Chronotide (`chrono-leviathan`) — aged-teal titan of the deep · Sage · major WIS / flaw DEX · Temporal Distortion / Age Reversal
Rose from the deep with its tentacles moving a half-second out of time, and the stirring rose
with it. Does not fight so much as arrive at the version of the match where it has already
won — Temporal Distortion is the polite word the stewards settled on.

### Umbratide (`cephalumbra`) — shadow-violet ink-pool · Rogue · major DEX / flaw CON · Phase Shift / Whip Strike
Pools like spilled ink and strikes from its own darkness — the deep's scout, the stewards
suspect. Phase Shifts through blows that have already landed; its match footage is the most
rewatched and least understood on the Circuit.

## Mythicals — *prestige body, Elite License*
### Arc: The Ones the Stories Were About
Every family has its legends. The Mythicals are what happens when the legends turn out to be
real, mildly annoyed at how the stories got told, and interested in setting the record
straight in person. The Elite License is less a permission than an acknowledgement.

### Titanus (`titanrex`) — grey-green plated apex bulk · Warrior · major STR · Prehistoric Roar / Unstoppable
The stories said something apex once walked the old world, and the stories undersold it. Plate
over the patience of extinct ages; holder of the Circuit's only standing ground-repair
invoice. Unstoppable is not marketing.

### Stellith (`stellarion`) — star-pale visitor, crown of horns · Ranger · major DEX · Stellar Shot / Cosmic Precision
Watches the way the night sky watches. The legends say it came down to see the arenas the
stories kept mentioning; the Stellar Shot suggests it intends to be worth the stories written
next.

### Maskelder (`wisdomkeeper`) — masked elder in ritual paint · Sage · major WIS · Foresight / Truth's Word
The mask is older than the league it fights in, and has never once come off. Reads matches
three moves before they happen — Foresight as a courtesy, Truth's Word as a verdict — and
every champion eventually makes the pilgrimage to lose to it on purpose.

### Magivex (`archmage-aleph`) — green conjurer, crooked violet hat · Wizard · major INT · Spell Echo / Arcane Mastery
First of the old order of arena mages; wrote the duelling forms every caster on the Circuit
still unknowingly uses. Has outlived the order, the forms, and most of the record books — the
Spell Echo means every casting is also a small history lesson.

### Aurelith (`harmonybringer`) — radiant gold floater · Bard · major CHA · Unison / Aegis Bond
The stories promised something radiant would come when the sport mattered enough, and Aurelith
took that as an invitation. Trails light like a hymn; fights by making its whole team briefly
legendary. Unison is the story, retold with the audience inside it.

## Saurians — *fusion: Mammal × Reptilian, bred in the Lab*
### Arc: Two Calendars
The Lab's first stable cross paired warm blood's urgency with cold blood's patience, and every
Saurian lives on both clocks at once — the mammal half already moving, the reptile half still
deciding whether the moment has arrived. The first generation is working out which calendar to
live by, in public, with an audience.

### Gillbrute (`grendscale`) — heavy green bruiser, gilled sea-cold stare · Warrior · Scaled Hide / Primal Roar
What took, when the first cross stabilised: reptilian armour under a Primal Roar from the warm
half of his ancestry. Runs on two calendars at once and has stopped apologising for either.

### Venimp (`vipramane`) — venom-green sprite · Rogue · Serpent's Strike / Mane Bristle
Got the serpent's strike and the mammal's restlessness, and no patience from anywhere. Small,
quick, and deeply unfair — the Mane Bristle is the only warning anyone gets, and it is not
much of one.

### Mossbrist (`thornhide`) — moss-brown bristle-orb · Tank · Thornplate / Ironscale
Inherited hide from one line and thorns from the other, and grew both until walking into him
became the whole argument. The Lab's proof that a defensive cross compounds: Thornplate over
Ironscale, patience over patience.

### Runeflight (`runewyrm`) — rune-violet flier · Sage · Runic Wisdom / Petrifying Gaze
Something in the cross woke an older script — sigils flicker along its wings that neither
parent line carries. Reads them mid-cast the way an heir reads a will: carefully, and with
growing interest in the Petrifying Gaze clause.

### Embersaur (`basilroar`) — rust-red saurian · Orator · Dread Bellow / Rally Cry
Took the bellow from the warm line and the audience from nowhere. The Dread Bellow empties the
front rows; the Rally Cry refills them. The Lab bred a bruiser and got an orator, and the
Circuit has decided not to correct the mistake.

## Tempestines — *fusion: Avian × Aquatic, bred in the Lab*
### Arc: Where Sky Meets Sea
Cross the scattering's fliers with the stirring's swimmers and you get weather with opinions.
The Tempestines inherited both parent families' crises — the empty sky and the restless deep —
and answered both the same way: by becoming the storm the two of them argue through.

### Stormtalon (`thunderoc`) — storm-gold terror of the high thermals · Ranger · Chain Lightning / Storm Dive
Dives with the Chain Lightning already committed — the sky's verdict, delivered before the
defence convenes.

### Galecrest (`galewing`) — storm-cyan flier · Rogue · Wind Veil / Squall
Rides gusts of its own making and is never quite where the last gust left it. The Wind Veil is
not evasion, strictly — it is the weather agreeing to stand between Galecrest and the
consequences.

### Tidegale (`tidecaller`) — sea-green caller of currents · Spellshield · Tidal Grace / Deluge
Speaks to currents in a murmur, and the tide listens — eventually, reluctantly, completely.
The Deluge arrives the way weather does: forecast by everyone, prevented by no one.

### Vortiqua (`maelstrom`) — deep-blue tentacled spiral · Wizard · Overcharge / Static Field
The cross at its most literal — sky-charge wrapped around an undertow. The water remembers
everywhere it has been; the Static Field makes sure the opponents do too.

### Brinefiend (`brinehowl`) — teal-skinned deep-trench horror · Orator · Gale Cry / Rally Squall
Carries the cold of the sea floor and a Gale Cry that tastes of salt at the back row. The deep
half mourns, the sky half rallies, and the Rally Squall is both at once — the Circuit's only
battle-hymn with an undertow.

## Broodkin — *fusion: Marsupial × Insectoid, bred in the Lab*
### Arc: The Fair That Never Leaves
The Lab crossed the fair's showmanship with the hive's numbers, and the result tours as one
act: performer and nest in the same body. Where the old colonies wound down and the fair kept
moving, the Broodkin are both answers at once — a colony that travels, a show that swarms.

### Jadewing (`chitinhop`) — jade-shelled skipper · Tank · Carapace / Dig In
The opening act: bounces between blows on quick glassy wings and Digs In the moment the act
needs a finale. The Carapace is real; the delight is realer.

### Broodclaw (`broodmother`) — plum-shelled matriarch · Sage · Brood Tend / Hive Mind
Travels with her nest, and her nest travels with the fair — the swarm strikes the tents,
raises the ring, and fills the cheap seats. Brood Tend runs the family; Hive Mind runs the
match.

### Huskthorn (`mantiskin`) — burnt-orange husk of dried thorns · Rogue · Ambush Fold / Blade Arms
The fair's knife-act gone feral — folds flat as a poster until the Ambush Fold opens, all
Blade Arms and no warning. What it lost in colour it kept in edge.

### Amberling (`resinback`) — stone-jointed golem sealed in amber · Spellshield · Resin Plate / Amber Set
The colony's mason once, joints sealed in the amber of old work. The fair found it holding up
a collapsed pavilion out of habit and hired it on the spot — Resin Plate for the crowd-surge,
Amber Set for the headliner who needs a wall.

### Swarmel (`swarmherd`) — a green mass agreeing to be one thing · Bard · Command Swarm / Disorient
Performs in the ring and mingles in the stands simultaneously, which the stewards have ruled
is one entrant. The Command Swarm keeps the vote unanimous — most of the time.
